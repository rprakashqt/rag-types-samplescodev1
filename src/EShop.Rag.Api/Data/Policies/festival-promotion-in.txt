eShop India Festival Promotion Terms

Festival promotions can provide temporary discounts, bank offers, coupon codes, or bundled accessories.

Unless a product is explicitly marked final sale, festival-discount purchases continue to use the normal product-category return policy.

Promotional cashback may be reversed when a returned order no longer satisfies the promotion conditions. Free bundled items must normally be returned together with the primary product when the refund calculation depends on the bundle.

The promotion terms do not override statutory consumer rights.

This sample policy is demonstration data for the RAG learning project.
