eShop Laptop Warranty Knowledge

New laptops sold directly by eShop include a standard manufacturer warranty unless the product listing states otherwise.

For the sample SKU-XPS-9530, the standard warranty period is one year from the invoice date. Manufacturing defects may be handled through the warranty process after return eligibility has expired.

Accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorized hardware modification, and normal battery wear are not covered by the standard manufacturer warranty unless an additional protection plan applies.

When a product is still inside the applicable return window and arrived damaged, the return or replacement process should normally be evaluated before the manufacturer-warranty process.
