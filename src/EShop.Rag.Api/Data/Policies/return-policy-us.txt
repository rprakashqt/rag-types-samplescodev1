eShop US Laptop Return Policy

Laptop computers purchased from eShop US may be returned within 14 calendar days from delivery when the product, original accessories, packaging, and proof of purchase are available.

Delivery-damage claims should be reported within 5 calendar days. Verified transit damage can qualify for replacement or refund.

Digital licenses and products damaged by the customer are excluded from the standard return process.

This sample policy is demonstration data for the RAG learning project and is not a real retailer policy.
