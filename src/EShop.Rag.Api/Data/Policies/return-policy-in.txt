eShop India Laptop Return Policy

Laptop computers purchased from eShop India may be returned within 30 calendar days from delivery when the product is in resalable condition with the original serial-number labels, accessories, charger, packaging, and invoice.

If the laptop is delivered damaged, the customer should report the damage within 7 calendar days and provide photographs of the package and product. A verified delivery-damage case can be handled as replacement or refund according to stock availability.

Software licenses, digitally delivered activation keys, and customer-caused physical damage are not covered by the standard return policy.

Festival-discount purchases follow the same 30-day return window unless the promotion terms explicitly mark the item as final sale.

This sample policy is demonstration data for the RAG learning project and is not a real retailer policy.
